Philosophy

WYSIWYG as moniker for popular processing systems (e.g., MS Word, Google Documents) is either a lie (because it shows codes during the editing process that will not print out) or it truly hides most structural and classification markers of the elements in documents such that maintaining the control over style is inversely correllated with the addition of content.

Code, even from its earliest until now, is a simple collection of unique tokens in which to identify separate contructs. Around 2020 it became fashionable to mock "woke" journalists (upon layoffs) to "learn to code", and while their retort to sociental oppression around gamer-gate and other political pressure is understandable, the term is a misnomer. More accurately, they should say "learn to comprehensively abstract constructs and elegantly re-compose them." Yet, I admit, it just doesn't fall off the tongue well. The point being that thing we come to all "coding" is more signifcantly about how we create a context of various elements of which to a assign unique codes.

A child will typically perform surface-level coding when describing observations, pulling from the limited set known constructs available to him: color, size, shape. Upon maturation into adulthood, more definitive yet obscure attributes facilitate efficient discourse: historical or regional nature, density, volume. Furthermore, the practioners of any subject matter will find great benefit in the ability converse over well-structured comprensive abstractions. Much ground can be covered quickly, when the interacting parties can express the prevelant features of their common domain as tersely as possible. Such an economy of words between professionals is powerful, yielding the opportunity idea exploration at the cutting edge.

Obviously, the notion of "coding" is even more profound among the interests of software engineers, as the contexts to be coded cuts accross all domains. In this way, you may consider a talented novelist to have a similar skill set: a strong ability to use prior knowledge to break many concepts down accross various domains. The quintessential "coder", software engineer, or novelist, in their roles, would ideally see in abstractions. Like Neo in "the Matrix", access to the underlying components to all matter will make it easy to manipulate that matter for whatever desired goal. Speaking of the "the Matrix", agents in the form of LLM chat boxes, can be added to this list of having a discrete form of this talent. In fact, 2025 has emerged as the year when sophisticated communication between engineers and "AI" really took off, with the advent of agentic systems. Now, the transferrence of analog and discrete abstractions, between man and machine, respectively, can greatly expedite the exploration of the certain knowledge frontiers: with machines maintaining a lossless grasp on the vast corpus of human knowledge, to allow the humans to wield their power in the continous spaces, where the status quo can be combinatorially be challenged.

Abstraction vs Immersion

How best to describe how things are constructed, as opposed to what resulting phenomena will emerge from the interactivity of those things is the premiere juxtaposition of any research. To speculate upon what features of interacting components can be adjusted to be more productive requires some understanding of how the existing state of features is creating the current production rate. This is where we start to consider "Performance as a Product" (i.e., PaaP) knowing that finding the new Meg Tilly will come from exhaustive re-modeling of constructs to a level that can only end with human mental effort, propped up by a discrete contextual knowledge base generated by AI. There is nothing more immersive than a young Meg Tilly; Ask Mr. Dresland when he meets Karin for the first time in "the girl in the swing". Only all of her components together could create the compelling effect it had on him such that he was ready to risk it all. Then, and only then, would he know the upper boundaries of the feelings that he could have for a person. It raises new questions. Could he feel more? Could his heart be sustatined feeling less? The abstractions do not answer these questions by themselves, only through exhaustive re-constructions can we know this.

Workspaces

Sessions are like workspaces that can maintain a set of files for editing.

Save a session
:mks! ~/.development.vim
:mksession! ~/.development.vim
:mksession! ~/.vim/sessions/session.vim
"session.vim" is named as example, but it would be best to use a more contextual name that represents the nature of the workspace activity (e.g., "development.vim", "research.vim", "loomis.vim").
Some use a "sessions" directory is added to seperate session files from other file types in that directory, but it is not necessary. While an alternative would be to store a workspace file at the user's home directory as a hidden file. That way you don't get result bloat from normal home directory interrogation, but you get a more easily accessible location for saving those files.
Restore that session
vim -S .development.vim
vim -S .vim/sessions/session.vim
